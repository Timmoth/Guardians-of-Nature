Become a volunteer

Your needs are at the heart of what we do. We use nature to heal and grow strength, and we're fully invested in the future of everyone who joins us. Volunteering here is a journey that helps you gain self-esteem, friendship and a sense of purpose — while learning about nature, planting, woodland management, carpentry, landscaping and conservation.

We're a very relaxed team. Do as much or as little as you like, and when you feel ready we offer accredited courses — there's never any pressure.

Families & youth

Mini Waste Warriors

It started when a child on our estate asked if we could run a litter pick for the kids. The response was so amazing we made it a regular event — and the Mini Waste Warriors were born.

It's an afternoon of fun for local children: keeping our green spaces tidy, learning about the dangers of dumping, and spotting plants and little critters along the way.

Your journey

The volunteer pathway

Participants can choose to follow our volunteer pathway, with four achievement points that support your goals. Move at your own pace — or not at all. It's all up to you.

1

Show up

Come to an open session, meet the team, and find your feet in the garden.

2

Get stuck in

Join a project — growing, building, litter picking or looking after our spaces.

3

Learn a skill

Take on a new area of interest and, when ready, an accredited course.

4

Lead & give back

Mentor newer volunteers and help shape the projects we take on next.
