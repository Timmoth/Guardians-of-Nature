Who we are

Nature is our greatest therapist.

We are a registered not-for-profit community interest company, dedicated to positive mental health and creating lasting friendships through nature-based activities across Penwortham and South Ribble. Our volunteers work together to support each other and preserve our green spaces.

We celebrate neurodiversity and welcome people navigating anxiety, PTSD, grief and loneliness. This is a safe space to use nature to heal, learn and make friends — young or old, and families are always welcome.

What we do

Healing, growing & giving back

From regenerating allotments to leading litter picks, every activity helps our people, our wildlife and our community grow. Here is a taste of what happens when we get our hands dirty.

Grow your own

We plant and harvest produce, raise seedlings, share plants and teach the community where food comes from.

Restore our spaces

We bring disused land back to life — woodland, gardens and wildflower areas that support local wildlife.

Support mental health

Gardening is our therapy. We build confidence, purpose and connection in a calm, judgement-free setting.

Teach & learn

Workshops, forest school and a volunteer pathway with real skills and accredited courses when you are ready.

Build community

Friendly, relaxed and inclusive — most of our best friendships started out here.

Come join us

Open sessions every week — bring your wellies and your curiosity.

Our projects

Rooted in the community

View the gallery

Alderfield Allotment

Our home base in Penwortham. Together we have regenerated this disused allotment, growing food, teaching local residents and youth where produce comes from, and creating a real sense of community spirit.

Alderfield, Penwortham · PR1 9HA

Woodland Nursery

With funding we built an education hut and a large polytunnel — an inclusive space for horticultural and conservation activities, partnered with Lancashire Adult Learning for courses and workshops. The Greencroft Valley woodlands next door are perfect for forest school.

Alderfield, Penwortham

Waste Warriors

Two teams of adults and youth meet regularly to clean up our streets and green spaces, looking after our wildlife and our neighbourhood. A lovely way to meet the team and get out in the fresh air.

Across Penwortham & South Ribble

Mini Waste Warriors

Born when a child on our estate asked to start a litter pick for kids. Now a regular monthly afternoon where local children learn to keep our green spaces tidy, spot plants and little critters, and help our wildlife.

Last Thursday of each month · 4:30pm

White Ribbon Garden

We are proud to maintain this garden of reflection at Kingsfold, supporting the White Ribbon Charity's aim to end violence against women and girls. White flowers, trees and shrubs carry a message of hope and change.

Kingsfold, Penwortham

Green Croft Valley

A special link to our home woodland and its wishing tree. Trusted by South Ribble Borough Council to care for the area, we build bird and bat boxes, wildlife piles, and use the space to educate about local wildlife.

Green Croft Valley, Penwortham
